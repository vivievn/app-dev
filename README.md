# My Favorite Movie: Ocean's 8

## About the Movie
**Ocean's 8** is a crime comedy film released in 2018, directed by Gary Ross. It follows Debbie Ocean, the sister of famous con artist Danny Ocean, as she plans an ambitious jewelry heist during the annual Met Gala in New York City.

Debbie gathers a team of seven skilled women to help steal a valuable diamond necklace worth millions of dollars. Together, they use their intelligence, creativity, and individual talents to carry out the perfect heist.

## Main Characters

- **Debbie Ocean** – The mastermind behind the jewelry heist.
- **Lou Miller** – Debbie's trusted friend and partner in planning the robbery.
- **Amita** – A skilled jeweler who helps with the plan.
- **Constance** – A talented pickpocket.
- **Nine Ball** – An expert hacker.
- **Tammy** – A former thief who helps organize the operation.
- **Rose Weil** – A fashion designer involved in the heist.
- **Daphne Kluger** – A famous actress who becomes part of their plan.

## Why I Like This Movie

I like *Ocean's 8* because of its clever plot, stylish outfits, and unexpected twists. I especially enjoy how each character has a unique skill that contributes to the success of the mission.

The movie also shows the importance of teamwork, confidence, intelligence, and careful planning. It is entertaining to watch how the team works together to pull off such an ambitious robbery.
